# Report template

Use exactly this structure. Replace everything in <angle brackets>. Keep the executive summary to 10 lines.

```markdown

**Window:** <since> to <until> (<N> months) | **Repos:** <names> | **Chat corpus:** <N conversations / none> (confidence: <low|med|high>)
**Not assessed:** <list>

## 1. Executive summary
<<= 10 lines: what the output looks like, what blocks the next level, the verdict.>

## 2. Scorecard
| # | Dimension | Score | Confidence | Evidence (2-3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
(10 rows; 3 = solid mid, 4 = senior, 5 = staff+)

**Overall placement:** <verdict> (weighting: <how>). <What pulls up / what pulls down.>
**Change since last evaluation:** <delta table or "first evaluation">

```

Rules: every claim cites evidence; facts and inferences are separate; no generic advice ("write more tests" must become "which tests, where, why"); flag any larger issue than the one asked about.
