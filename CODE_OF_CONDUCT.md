# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a [security advisory](https://github.com/Remiiiiii/Senior-Dev-Coach/security/advisories/new) or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content and ban contributors who violate these standards.
